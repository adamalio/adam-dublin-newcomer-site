# Build brief: Adam's Dublin Newcomer Field Guide

Goal: Create a polished, mobile-first static website for Adam, who is relocating Hamburg → Dublin for Google. It should feel useful, practical, not overwhelming, and work as a one-page "newcomer command center".

Audience: Adam, a new Google Dublin arrival who wants social landing, football club/community, practical setup, and fun facts.

Tone: friendly, modern, concise, numbers/data/facts where possible. Keep it stepwise and prioritized.

Content requirements:
- Hero: "Dublin Landing Kit" or similar.
- Include quick stats / facts:
  - Dublin is Ireland's capital and largest city.
  - Google Dublin is centered around Grand Canal Dock / Barrow Street area.
  - Leap Card works across Dublin Bus, Luas, DART and commuter rail in Dublin area.
  - Dublin.ie says new arrivals can apply for PPS number online after arrival and usually need proof of address, photo ID, and evidence of why they need one.
  - Dublin.ie recommends Meetup and InterNations for meeting people / fellow nationals.
  - Dublin.ie notes Leap fares can be up to 31% cheaper than single tickets (from search result; mark as source: Dublin.ie getting around).
- Sections:
  1. First 7 days: sleep base, SIM/bank, Leap Card, work commute, first social event.
  2. Social landing: 3 recurring social touchpoints per week for 6 weeks = 18 contact points; target 5 repeat contacts.
  3. Football path: start with casual 5-a-side / GoodRec / Meetup / Google internal groups, then club trials/Sunday league.
  4. Communities and groups: Meetup Dublin, New in Dublin, InterNations, German/expat communities, tech/product/AI meetups, running/climbing/gym groups.
  5. Neighborhood shortlist around Google/Grand Canal Dock: Grand Canal Dock, Ringsend, Sandymount, Ballsbridge, Ranelagh, Rathmines, Portobello, Dublin 6/8. Add commuter notes.
  6. Fun facts / culture: pubs as social spaces, GAA is culturally huge, weather/sea walks, Dublin is compact but housing is competitive.
  7. Links/resources with source labels.
- Include "Next best step" card: choose temporary accommodation window + first football/social event.
- Include interactive checklist with localStorage.
- Include filters/buttons for categories if simple.
- Must be static: index.html, styles.css, app.js. No build step.
- Must be beautiful: glass cards, gradient, responsive, clean typography, emojis ok but not too much.
- Include source links visibly:
  - Dublin.ie relocating: https://dublin.ie/live/relocating-to-dublin/
  - Dublin.ie move 10 steps: https://dublin.ie/live/relocating-to-dublin/move-to-dublin-10-steps/
  - Dublin.ie meeting people: https://dublin.ie/live/relocating-to-dublin/meeting-people-city/
  - Leap Card Dublin: https://about.leapcard.ie/dublin
  - Leap Visitor Card: https://about.leapcard.ie/leap-visitor-card
  - Meetup Dublin: https://www.meetup.com/find/ie--dublin/
  - Meetup 5-a-side: https://www.meetup.com/topics/5-a-side/ie/
  - GoodRec Dublin Soccer: https://www.goodrec.com/soccer-leagues/dublin
  - Soccer Ireland Dublin directory: https://www.soccer-ireland.com/dublin-soccer/index.htm

Verification:
- Site opens locally without console errors.
- Files exist: index.html, styles.css, app.js.
- No external build dependencies.
- Do not include secrets.

Work mode:
- Treat this as a goal loop: after writing, self-review against the brief and fix gaps before final response.
