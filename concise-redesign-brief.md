# Concise redesign brief: important only

Adam feedback: better visual direction, but still too much fluff / non-important stuff. The site must be concise and only show important/actionable content.

Goal: Keep the premium/esoteric beauty, but turn the page into a tight decision artifact. Remove decorative sections that do not help Adam act.

Principles:
- One screen should answer: What should Adam do first? Where should he live/search? How should he meet people? How does he play football? What admin matters?
- Cut poetic copy unless it also clarifies action.
- Keep the astrolabe style, but use it as information architecture, not decoration.
- Make every card/action earn its place.

Required final structure:
1. Hero: beautiful, concise, with 3 hard priorities only.
   - Priority 1: Housing base near reliable Barrow Street / Grand Canal Dock commute.
   - Priority 2: Admin unlocks: phone/data, Leap, bank/address docs, PPS evidence.
   - Priority 3: Social/football: one recurring room per week.
2. Action board: exactly 6 high-signal actions. Each with why, link/evidence, time window.
3. Dublin compass: concise area matrix only for 5 areas max; rank by usefulness.
4. Social/football engine: a single visual system showing 18 contact points over 6 weeks + football ladder.
5. Checklist: reduce from 12 to 8 important items max.
6. Sources: keep only strongest source links. No decorative culture cards unless directly useful.

Cut/remove:
- "practical spellbook" fluff language if not needed.
- Culture cards: pubs/GAA/coast/housing unless converted into actionable notes.
- Any duplicate content repeated in hero, facts, checklist, and sections.
- Long labels and poetic headings that obscure the action.

Visual requirements:
- Still beautiful/dark/premium.
- Less vertical sprawl.
- More information compression: cards, ranked matrix, chart.
- Use numbers: 6 weeks, 18 contact points, 5 areas, 8 checklist items.

Verification:
- JS syntax passes.
- Page contains no more than 8 checklist inputs.
- Page contains no more than 8 <p> tags.
- Live publish after local verification.
